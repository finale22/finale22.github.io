2026-07-06 ~ 07-11

I attended ICML 2026 in Seoul!

It was my first time attending an international conference, and I was so excited that I could hardly sleep. 😂 The first day felt a little awkward, but as I gradually adjusted to the conference atmosphere, I was able to have a meaningful experience throughout the week.

This conference gave me a great deal of motivation for research. I found myself wanting to become a researcher who can offer meaningful insights and perspectives to others through my work.

My paper was accepted to the Graph Foundation Models workshop at ICML 2026, and I gave a poster presentation on Friday. I was surprised that more researchers than I expected asked questions about my work, and I was very happy to discuss my research with them. 😊
