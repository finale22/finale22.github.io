2026-06-23 ~ 06-26

I visited Jeju to attend the Korea Computer Congress 2026.

It was a meaningful experience to listen to and learn about a wide range of research from many people.

During the conference, I gave an oral presentation for the first time. I was very nervous, but thankfully, I was able to finish it without any problems. 😊

Since my presentation was scheduled for the final day, I did not have much time for sightseeing. Still, I was happy to catch a glimpse of Jeju's beautiful sea and enjoy some delicious local food.
